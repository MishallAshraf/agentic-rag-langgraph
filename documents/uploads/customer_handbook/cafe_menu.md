Dietary labels used on this menu: (V) vegetarian, (VG) vegan, (GF) made without gluten-containing ingredients. Our kitchen handles gluten, nuts, dairy, eggs, soy and sesame, so we cannot guarantee any dish is completely free of an allergen. See the Allergens section for details.

## Allergens

We follow labelling rules for the 14 major allergens: celery, cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, soy and sulphites. An allergen folder at the counter lists the allergens in every dish and drink, and it is updated whenever a recipe changes.

Key points:
- **Nuts:** the almond croissant, granola, carrot cake, almond milk and the peanut butter smoothie contain nuts. All other dishes are made in the same kitchen, so traces of nuts are possible.
- **Gluten:** dishes marked (GF) are made without gluten-containing ingredients, and gluten-free bread is toasted separately. The kitchen is not gluten-free, so we cannot guarantee there is no cross-contact. Fries are cooked in a shared fryer with battered fish.
- **Dairy:** lactose-free milk and four plant milks are available. Dishes marked (VG) contain no dairy.
- **Sesame:** the falafel bowl's tahini dressing and hummus contain sesame.

Anyone with a severe allergy should tell staff before ordering, so the kitchen can take extra care and staff can check the allergen folder with them.
