# Harbour Bean Café Customer Guidelines

These guidelines explain the café's policies and how to answer customer questions. They are used by staff at the counter and on the phone, and by anyone replying to messages, emails and online reviews. If a question is not covered here, say so honestly and offer to find out from the manager on duty.

## How to reply to customers

- Be warm, clear and brief. Greet the customer, answer the question first, then add anything useful.
- Use the customer's name if you know it. Sign messages with your first name and "Harbour Bean Café".
- Give exact facts from the menu and these guidelines: prices, times and allergens. Never guess. If you are not sure, say you will check and get back to them, and give a time.
- Reply to messages and emails within 24 hours, and to online reviews within 48 hours.
- Never promise anything these guidelines do not allow, such as a discount, a free meal or a reserved table on a busy weekend. Offer to pass the request to the manager instead.
- Do not share personal details of other customers or staff, and do not discuss staff by name in public replies.

## Allergy and dietary questions

Allergy questions must always be answered carefully, because a wrong answer can make someone seriously ill.

- Answer from the allergen information on the menu and in the allergen folder. Never say a dish is "safe" or "allergen-free". Our kitchen handles gluten, nuts, dairy, eggs, soy and sesame, so cross-contact is always possible.
- For a severe allergy (for example nuts or coeliac disease), recommend that the customer tells staff before ordering, so the kitchen can take extra care and staff can check the allergen folder with them.
- If a customer has had an allergic reaction in the café, call emergency services immediately if they have trouble breathing, swelling of the face or throat, or feel faint. Then tell the manager and record it in the incident book.
- Vegetarian (V), vegan (VG) and gluten-free (GF) options are marked on the menu. Vegan customers can ask for any espresso drink with oat, almond, soy or coconut milk.

## Reservations and seating

- We take table reservations for groups of 6 to 16 people only, by phone or email, at least 24 hours in advance. Smaller groups cannot book; tables are first come, first served.
- On weekends between 9:00 and 13:00, reserved tables are held for 15 minutes after the booking time, then released.
- For groups of more than 16, or to book the whole café for a private event, the customer should email the manager. Private hire is available on weekday evenings after 17:00.
- During busy weekend hours, we ask groups to keep tables to 90 minutes so other customers can be seated.
- The terrace cannot be reserved.

## Orders, payment and refunds

- Customers order and pay at the counter; we bring food to the table. We accept cards, contactless and mobile payments, and cash. There is no minimum card spend.
- We do not add a service charge. Tips are optional and are shared equally between all staff.
- If a dish or drink is wrong or not up to standard, we will remake it or refund it straight away, with no questions asked. The customer just needs to tell staff while they are in the café.
- For complaints after the visit, ask for the date, time and what was ordered, apologise, and pass it to the manager, who replies within 2 working days. The manager may offer a refund or a voucher; staff should not promise either.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 3 to 5 working days to appear on card statements.
- Gift cards can be bought in the café in any amount from $10 to $100. They are valid for 12 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Takeaway, delivery and keep cups

- Every drink and most of the menu is available to take away. Takeaway prices are the same as dining in.
- Customers who bring their own reusable cup get $0.30 off any hot drink.
- Takeaway cups are compostable. We do not charge for takeaway packaging.
- We deliver through local delivery apps between 8:00 and 15:00. Delivery prices are set by the app and can be higher than in the café. Problems with a delivery order should be reported through the app first; if the app cannot help, the customer can email us with the order number.
- Phone orders for collection are welcome for orders of 3 or more items. We confirm a collection time when the order is placed.

## Loyalty card

- Customers get a stamp for every hot drink. After 9 stamps, the 10th hot drink is free (any size, any extras).
- Stamps are collected on a paper card or in our app. Paper cards and app stamps cannot be combined.
- Lost paper cards cannot be replaced, but app stamps are kept on the customer's account.
- Free drinks cannot be exchanged for food or cash.

## Catering and whole cakes

- Whole cakes (carrot, lemon drizzle, chocolate brownie tray, or vegan chocolate and orange) can be ordered with 48 hours' notice. An 8-inch cake serves 10 to 12 people and costs $38. Gluten-free and vegan options are available for most cakes; ask when ordering.
- Personalised writing on a cake is free, up to 20 characters.
- Office catering (sandwich platters, pastries and coffee flasks) is available for 10 people or more, with 3 working days' notice. A sandwich platter for 10 costs $95. Coffee flasks serve 10 cups and cost $28.
- Orders are confirmed by email and paid in advance. Cancellations more than 24 hours before collection get a full refund; later cancellations are not refunded.

## Facilities and accessibility

- Free Wi-Fi is available; the network name and password are printed on the receipt. During busy weekend hours, laptops are welcome at the bar seating by the window rather than at large tables.
- The café is step-free from Quay Street, with a level entrance and an accessible toilet with grab rails. One table near the entrance is kept clear for wheelchair users when possible.
- Baby changing facilities are in the accessible toilet. High chairs are available.
- Well-behaved dogs on a lead are welcome on the terrace and inside at the tables near the door. We provide water bowls and dog biscuits. Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere in the café.
- Phone chargers can be borrowed at the counter.

## Lost property, events and jobs

- Lost property is kept for 30 days. Customers can call or email with a description and the date of their visit. Phones, wallets and keys are kept in the safe.
- Every first Thursday of the month, we host a live acoustic evening from 18:00 to 20:30. Entry is free, but the café asks for a reservation because seats are limited.
- For jobs, applicants should email their CV to the manager. We hire baristas and kitchen staff, especially before the summer season. Training is provided.
- Media, partnerships and supplier enquiries go to the manager by email.
